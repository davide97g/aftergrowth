# Ink Magic — design v0.1

Inspired by *Witch Hat Atelier* (Kamome Shirahama): magic is not a power you have, it is a drawing
you make correctly. Our glyph shapes are original; the structure borrows the manga's spirit.

## What the manga does (research notes)
- All a spell needs is **special ink** and a **seal**: a circle that contains the spell.
- A **sigil** inside the seal decides the element. Five families: fire, water, earth, wind, light.
- **Signs** around the sigil shape the effect: direction, motion, strength. Drawing an arrow the wrong
  way sends you the wrong way; precision matters.
- A glyph **activates when the circle is closed**. Pre-drawn glyphs can sit dormant on cloth, soles,
  paper, objects (contraptions).
- Rules: never cast on a body (healing included), never let the unknowing see you draw.
- The craft is the point: witches get "stuck" like artists, invent new glyphs, combine signs.

## Our rules
1. Press **E** (or the pen button) to uncap the pen. The world keeps living; the witch kneels to draw.
2. Draw **sigils** first, anywhere on screen. Each sigil is one stroke:

   | Sigil | Shape | Element | Effect |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Growth | spiral | earth | flowers bloom, crops grow a stage |
   | Water | wave ~ | water | a small rain cloud waters the area |
   | Wind | curl (a line with one loop) | wind | a gust in the line's direction, windmill races |
   | Light | five-point star | light | a floating light that lasts the night |
   | Flame | triangle | fire | a cozy campfire |

3. **Signs** change the spell:
   - **dots** (taps): each one makes the spell bigger and longer, up to three
   - a **straight line**: points the spell (wind blows that way)
4. **Close the seal**: draw a circle around everything. The spell fires the moment it closes, on the
   ground beneath the circle. The glyph burns into the grass in gold ink, then fades.
5. Mistakes have consequences, never punishment:
   - no circle: nothing happens (just ink)
   - an empty seal or an unclear mark: the ink smudges and fizzles
   - more than two sigils: the seal is overcrowded and fizzles
   - circle closed over the sky: the ink drips off the island
6. **Two sigils in one seal combine.** Some pairs make something new, some argue:
   - water + light: rainbow
   - growth + water: bloom (rain, flowers, and a new sapling)
   - wind + light: firefly dance
   - water + flame: steam (cancels)
   - other pairs: the sigils argue (fizzle)
7. The **Grimoire** (G) is the research journal. Growth and water are known from your master's
   notes; the others start as riddles. Every result is recorded, failures included, because
   failures are research too.

## Ink (built)
- The bottle holds 12 drops. Each sigil costs one, each dot one more; a smudged glyph spills one.
- Pick meadow flowers (they regrow after 8 game hours) and brew them in the cauldron by the
  cottage: 2 flowers make 3 drops.
- Not enough ink: the pen runs dry and nothing is spent.

## Contraptions (built)
- Three carving stones: above the crop rows, by the greenhouse, by the portal.
- Close a seal over a stone and the glyph is carved in instead of cast, for double the ink.
- The stone then repeats it: water every 4 game hours, growth every 6, wind every 3; light and
  firefly stones relight every night; flame stones keep their fire going.
- A new glyph closed over a carved stone replaces the old one.

## Later
- Ink quality: flower colour changes the ink (gold ink from yellow flowers is stronger).
- Contraptions on objects: watering can, scarecrow, lantern.
- Glyph precision score: neater circles = stronger spells.
- Shared seals in multiplayer: one witch draws the sigil, another closes the circle.
