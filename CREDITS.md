The theme song "Seeds on the Wind" is an original composition, synthesised by `src/audio/theme.ts`
(rendered copy in `art/music/`).

Everything else (the chibi witch via `tools/blender/build_chibi.py`, islands, egg cottage, greenhouse, leaf windmill, round and cypress trees, flowers,
bunny, lantern, bridge, portal, clouds, birds, cat, cauldron, mailbox, fireplace, music, crystals, roots, well, beehive, bench, swing, scarecrow, leaf solar lamp, rain barrel, vine arch, gazebo, flower bed, lamp post, crystal lamp, campfire) is modelled procedurally by `tools/blender/build_props.py`
or in code. Concept art in `art/concept/` was generated with Codex image generation; prompts sit next
to each image.
